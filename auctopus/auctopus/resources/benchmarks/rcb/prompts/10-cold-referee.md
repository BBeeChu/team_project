1. Summary of the claims (three to five sentences).
2. Strengths.
3. Weaknesses, each tied to a specific place in the manuscript, covering: whether the evidence supports the claims, whether the predictions were stated before the experiment and compared honestly, whether baselines and controls are adequate, statistical soundness, clarity, and reproducibility from what is written.
4. Questions for the authors.
5. Requested changes, numbered.

End with two lines:
Recommendation: one of Reject, Major revision, Minor revision, Accept.
Confidence: an integer from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
