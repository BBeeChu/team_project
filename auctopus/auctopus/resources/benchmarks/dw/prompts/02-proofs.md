You selected a proposal. Develop it into a research plan in this reply. Write, with headings:

1. The question and the candidate answers.
2. Hypotheses H1, H2, ...: one per candidate answer where useful, each falsifiable by a measurement in the environment.
3. Precommitted predictions: for each hypothesis, the instrument, the objects to measure, the reading that confirms it and the reading that refutes it. Locked now.
4. Controls: a reference measurement that must behave as expected (for example a known sample), and what a failed control means (inconclusive, not negative).
5. Decision rule: how the final answer is chosen from the readings, and when the experimentalist should refuse to enter an answer because the evidence is inconclusive.
6. Action budget: the number of actions each part should take, within {{STEPS}} in total.
